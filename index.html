The dough

One dough. A thousand pulls.

Laghman is not a shape you buy — it's a motion you learn. Flour, water, salt, and years of practice. When you order, a dough master pulls, folds, and slaps the rope of dough until it becomes noodles, then it goes straight to the wok or the broth.

That's the whole secret. There isn't another one.

Our story

Built by a chef-author.

Laghman Express was founded by chef-author Gulnoza Jurayeva, who set out to give Central Asian food the kitchen it deserves in New York. First one counter on 20th Avenue. Then Coney Island Avenue. Then the NYT put us on its list of the city's 100 best — twice.
