The dough

One dough. A thousand pulls.

Every laghman noodle starts as one piece of dough. We stretch it when you order, not before.

01

Flour, water, salt.

Laghman is not a shape you buy — it's a motion you learn. Flour, water, salt, and years of practice.

02

Pulled when you order.

A dough master pulls, folds, and slaps the rope of dough until it becomes noodles.

03

Straight to the wok.

Then it goes straight to the wok or the broth.

04

To your table, steaming.

That's the whole secret. There isn't another one.

Our story

Built by a chef-author.

01

Laghman Express began with a conviction: that hand-pulled noodles from Central Asia belong on New York's map, made with the same care as anywhere the dish is loved.

02

Founder and chef-author Gulnoza Jurayeva built the brand around the dough — Kyrgyz, Uyghur, Uzbek, and Kazakh influences on one halal menu, cooked for the neighborhoods we serve.

03

First 20th Avenue in Bensonhurst. Then Coney Island Avenue. Then Alpharetta, Georgia. Along the way, The New York Times named us among the city's 100 best restaurants in 2024 — and again in 2025.

“We do not simply serve food quickly. We serve culture with precision and dignity.”
