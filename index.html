Three movements to the table.

The room

Somebody's grandmother would recognise this table.

Laghman Express began with a conviction: that hand-pulled noodles from Central Asia belong on New York's map — made with the same care on 20th Avenue as anywhere the dish is loved.

The kitchens are built around the dough itself — Kyrgyz, Uyghur, Uzbek and Kazakh influences, cooked for the neighbourhoods outside the door.

From 20th Avenue in Bensonhurst to Coney Island Avenue, Emmons Avenue and Alpharetta, the team carries the same standards into every kitchen. The New York Times named Laghman Express among the city's 100 best in 2024, 2025 and 2026.
